# User guide

## Overview

N-ATLAS Civic Evidence helps authorized reviewers organize testimony and incident
reports into reviewable, source-linked records. Extracted claims remain
`unverified` until a human reviewer acts on them.

## Tasks

1. **Open a case.** Use Cases to find authorized cases, or create a new one and
   record consent.
2. **Review evidence.** Compare each extracted claim with its transcript segment
   and audio range.
3. **Decide.** Accept, edit, reject, or flag each claim and add a note.
4. **Consult civic guidance.** Look up official procedural sources. Guidance is
   kept separate from witness statements.
5. **Export a report.** Preview the assembled report and export it once at least
   one claim has been reviewed.

## Labels

- `Model-extracted` - produced by N-ATLAS, not yet reviewed.
- `Human-reviewed` - confirmed or edited by a reviewer.
- `Unverified` - not independently corroborated.

## Cautions

- The system documents; it does not determine whether an allegation is true.
- Civic guidance is informational, not individualized legal advice.
- If something looks wrong or a model result is missing, flag it rather than
  assuming it is correct.
